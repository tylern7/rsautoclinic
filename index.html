About RS Auto Clinic

Family-owned. Honest. Since 2014.

Named after its founder Ron Snell, RS Auto Clinic is a family-owned and operated shop serving drivers and businesses throughout Burlington, Oakville and surrounding communities. As a small, local business we offer great rates and personal service, and we always take the time to explain what service is needed and why.

Backed by 25+ years of experience, we test, verify and repair the root cause, so you leave safer, more confident, and without paying for guesswork.
